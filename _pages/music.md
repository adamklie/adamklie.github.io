I've played guitar since I was a kid. For a long time it was a private thing — playing by myself, learning covers, occasionally recording a rough demo into my phone. Recently that's been changing.

## Ozone Destructors

I play rhythm/lead guitar in a trio called **Ozone Destructors** with two friends in San Diego. Sural is the frontman and producer; Greg holds down the rhythm section. We're three people with flexible instrumentation — any duo or the full trio can play a gig with different arrangements. We've been practicing roughly weekly since mid-2025, recording every session on a GoPro and archiving the audio.

We've worked through a pretty wide repertoire — plenty of covers (Weezer, Radiohead, Sublime, Nirvana, White Stripes, Amy Winehouse) and a growing set of originals. The goal is to tighten up a 12–15 song core setlist and start playing gigs.

## Solo

Acoustic covers, open mics, and the occasional original. Most of what I'm working on solo lives in my music directory as rough takes; the best ones eventually get re-recorded as proper demos.

## Sural

A collaboration project with Sural that predates the band — a mix of originals and collabs with other musicians. A lot of it is electronic and production-heavy, which is a fun change from the mostly-live setup of Ozone Destructors.

## Greenroom

Because my music library had grown past what I could manage by hand, I built [Greenroom](/projects/greenroom/) — a self-hosted career manager for aspiring musicians. Track repertoire, rate practice takes, plan content, and follow a roadmap from bedroom to stage. It reads my existing directory structure and turns it into a dashboard.
