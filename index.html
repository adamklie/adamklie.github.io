Adam Klie

Co-Founder & Head of Technology, Gaius Therapeutics · Computational biologist & ML researcher · adam@gaius.bio

La Jolla, CA

I’m the co-founder and Head of Technology of Gaius Therapeutics, an early-stage biotech company that uses a machine learning–guided “genetic switch” discovery platform to design in vivo cell therapies precise enough to treat autoimmune disease.

I completed my PhD in the Bioinformatics and Systems Biology (BISB) program at UC San Diego in 2025 in Professor Hannah Carter’s Lab, and continued as a Schmidt AI Postdoctoral Fellow before co-founding Gaius.

My expertise sits at the intersection of gene regulation, single-cell biology, and deep learning — I build machine learning models that decode how the regulatory genome shapes cell biology. Along the way I created EUGENe and several open-source tools, alongside my publications.

Outside of work, I’ve lived a couple of other lives worth their own pages: a decade of competitive basketball — UC San Diego, professional ball in Canada and Germany, and coaching — and an ongoing life in music. I also write about all of it.
